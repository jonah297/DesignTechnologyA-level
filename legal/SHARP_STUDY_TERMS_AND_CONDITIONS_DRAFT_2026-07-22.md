## 4. Student Data Visibility and Linked-Class Use

### 4.1 Why This Section Matters

Sharp Study is designed so that authorised teachers can support students by seeing relevant educational progress data. This section explains what happens when a Student User links their account to a class, teacher, school, institution, or licence.

### 4.2 Independent Student Data

If a Student User uses Sharp Study independently and is not linked to a class or institution, Teacher Users do not receive a teacher-dashboard view of that student's individual progress.

If an Independent Student User later joins a class or institution, data generated after joining, and some existing account information needed to operate the class link, may become visible to authorised Teacher Users as described below.

### 4.3 Linked Student Data Visible to Teachers

When a Student User joins a class or institution, authorised Teacher Users and Account Managers for that class or institution may be able to see data including:

- the student's display name;
- the student's school email address where needed for identity, class management, or account support;
- class membership and approved-student status;
- assignment status, including not started, started, complete, incomplete, overdue, or completed late;
- assignment deadlines, attempts, target mastery, completion percentage, and completion timestamps;
- topic progress, chapter progress, subsection progress, mastery estimates, and memory-decay status;
- quiz attempts, answer outcomes, written-answer scores, and mark estimates;
- streaks, XP, active engagement counts, rank, and leaderboard position where enabled;
- last active indicators, study activity, and learning-session timestamps;
- reminder, reward, support, and nudge indicators generated by the App;
- content flags or marking-review reports, but new content feedback is designed to be anonymous where possible.

This data is referred to in these Terms as "Student Performance Data".

### 4.4 Purpose of Teacher Visibility

Teacher Users may use Student Performance Data only for legitimate educational purposes, such as:

- monitoring learning progress;
- identifying topics that may need reteaching or support;
- checking whether assignments have been attempted or completed;
- supporting students who are falling below target;
- planning lessons, interventions, revision, feedback, or conversations with students and parents;
- reviewing content quality or marking behaviour.

Teacher Users must not use Student Performance Data for unrelated purposes, personal curiosity, public embarrassment, unfair treatment, or any purpose that breaches school policy, safeguarding rules, employment duties, or data-protection law.

### 4.5 Teacher and Institution Responsibility

Teacher Users and Institution Users are responsible for:

- ensuring they are authorised to use Sharp Study with their students;
- ensuring students and, where appropriate, parents or guardians are given clear information about the use of Sharp Study;
- using Student Performance Data in accordance with their school or institution's policies;
- complying with applicable data-protection, safeguarding, equality, education, employment, and record-keeping obligations;
- managing which teachers have access to which classes;
- removing access when a student or teacher should no longer be linked to a class.

### 4.6 Not Sole Evidence for Punishment or Formal Decisions

Sharp Study data may be useful evidence, but it may be incomplete, delayed, affected by technical issues, or based on automated scoring. Teacher Users and Institution Users must not rely on Sharp Study as the sole source of evidence for serious disciplinary, safeguarding, academic, or welfare decisions.

For example, if Sharp Study shows that homework was late, incomplete, or not started, a teacher should use professional judgement and consider context before applying any sanction or consequence.

### 4.7 Class Codes and Approved Student Lists

Students may be required to use a class code and an approved school email before joining a class. A class code controls joining only. Once a student has successfully joined a class, the student may retain class access unless removed by an authorised Teacher User or Account Manager.

If a student is removed from a class, they may lose access to that class's assignments, teacher dashboard link, and class leaderboard. They may be able to rejoin later if a teacher provides a fresh valid join code and the student remains approved.
